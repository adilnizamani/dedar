Privacy policy

Dedar: Sindhi in Devanagari · Last updated: 14 October 2026

In short

Dedar does not collect, store or share any of your personal data.

Everything you do in the app stays on your own phone.

What stays on your phone

To remember your place, Dedar saves a few things in your phone's own storage:

  • Your finished lessons and where you left off
  • The names and topi pictures of the learners you add
  • Your settings, like text size and calm mode
  • Voice recordings, but only if you use the recording studio

This information never leaves your phone. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Microphone

Dedar only asks for the microphone in the recording studio, and only when you tap record.

Your recordings stay on your phone. They only leave it if you choose to export and share them yourself.

What Dedar does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No ads
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No selling or sharing of data

Where the app comes from

The app's files are loaded from GitHub Pages, a website hosting service. Like any website, GitHub may record basic technical details, such as your IP address, when your phone downloads the files. Dedar does not see or use this information.

You can read GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

Dedar is made for teens and adults. It is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

  • To clear one learner's progress: open Settings in the app and use Start over.
  • To remove a learner: open Settings and tap Remove next to their name.
  • To delete everything: uninstall the app, or clear its data in your phone's settings.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
